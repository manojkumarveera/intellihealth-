        Proposed System Architecture & Core Modules

        Seven decoupled, independently scalable functional modules built with HL7 FHIR contracts as detailed in Section III of the research paper.

        PostgreSQL Structured Engine

        Guarantees strict ACID transactional consistency and referential integrity for core relational FHIR resources including Patient, Encounter, and MedicationRequest. Essential for medical auditing and billing.

        MongoDB High-Volume Engine

        Accommodates rapid, schema-flexible time-series streams originating from continuous IoT biometric sensors, raw BioBERT embedding vectors, and uncurated clinical text payloads without table locks.

        Data Security, Privacy & Regulatory Compliance Vault

        Healthcare data protection strictly engineered under India's DPDP Act 2023, ABDM guidelines, HIPAA Security Rule, and EU GDPR.

        Cryptographic Encryption

        TLS 1.3 enforced for all data in flight between wearables, hospitals, and servers. AES-256 at rest with HSM-separated key rotation.

        Role-Based Access (RBAC)

        Strict least-privilege enforcement. Receptionists cannot access clinical labs; doctors view only assigned cohort patients.

        Immutable Cryptographic Audit Trail

        Every view, export, and inference action is permanently hashed and logged to guarantee non-repudiation in malpractice reviews.

        DPDP Act 2023 & ABDM Consent

        Patient-centric consent management: Patients authorize or revoke institutional data sharing with a single click via ABDM ABHA ID.

        Privacy-Preserving AI

        De-identified training corpora, differential privacy perturbation, and federated learning allowing multi-hospital model training without raw data leaving premises.

        Automatic PHI De-identification

        Real-time NLP redaction removes HIPAA Safe Harbor 18 direct identifiers (names, MRNs, phone numbers, exact dates) before model inference.

        Intelli Health 360 Research Paper Abstract

        Today, a patient's health information is scattered across hospitals, labs, pharmacies, and wearable devices, so doctors often have to piece the picture together by hand, which slows decisions and raises the risk of mistakes. IntelliHealth 360 addresses this by bringing a patient's records into one secure digital health profile built on the HL7 FHIR standard, so different systems can share data smoothly. On top of this unified record, Machine Learning predicts health risks, Natural Language Processing (NLP) turns long medical reports into short, readable summaries, and Explainable AI (XAI) shows the reasoning behind each prediction so clinicians can understand it and decide how far to trust it.
